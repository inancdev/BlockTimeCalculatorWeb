FAQ

How does the input work?

Digits fill in right-to-left. The display starts at 00:00; each key press shifts the value left — press 4, 1, 5 to enter 04:15. No colon key needed.

How is the math different from a normal calculator?

Block Time uses 60-minute rollovers. 01:45 + 00:30 equals 02:15, not 01:75.

What happens after I press equals?

The calculation is saved to the history feed and the total stays in the display, so you can keep adding the next leg. Press C to clear.

Can I reuse a past result?

Yes. Tap any row in the history feed to copy that total back into the active display.
