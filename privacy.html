Privacy Policy

App: Block Time

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Summary

Block Time is a time calculator for pilots. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data We Collect

None. Block Time does not collect personal information of any kind.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following locally on your device only:

  • Calculation history — the equations and totals you compute.
  • Theme preference — your light/dark theme choice.

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Network Use

Block Time does not connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that transmit data.

Third Parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any.

Children's Privacy

Block Time does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new "Last updated" date.
